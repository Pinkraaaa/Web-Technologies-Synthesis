How to test

  1. Keyboard: unplug your mouse and press Tab through every page.
  2. Zoom: zoom your browser to 200% and check nothing is cut off.
  3. Automatic check: run the page through WAVE or Lighthouse.
  4. Screen reader: listen to one page with NVDA (Windows) or VoiceOver (Mac and iPhone).
  5. Real people: ask someone else to use the site and watch where they get stuck.

Words to know

Alt text
A written description of an image, added with the alt attribute.
Captions
On-screen text of speech and important sounds in a video.
Contrast ratio
How different the text colour is from the background, from 1:1 to 21:1.
Screen reader
Software that reads a page aloud or sends it to a braille display.
WCAG
The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, the main international standard for accessible websites.
