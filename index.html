    Test your own site

Don't believe it?
Try it right now.

Three thirty-second experiments. No special software needed.

  • Put the mouse down

    Press Tab a few times. Can you always see where you are? On many sites you can't. That is daily life for keyboard users.

  • Zoom to 200%

    Press Ctrl and + five times on your favourite website. Does anything get cut off or overlap?

  • Mute, then watch

    Turn the sound off and play any news video. Without captions, how much do you actually understand?

How each page works

  1. Use the small website on the page in Barrier mode and notice what feels hard or missing.
  2. Flip the switch to Fixed. Click it, or press Tab to reach it and Space to change it.
  3. Read the numbered notes to see what changed, then scroll down for tips and the code behind each fix.
