Low vision

Light grey text, tiny fonts and pictures with no description make a page hard or impossible to use for people with low vision, and for anyone using a screen reader. Here is a made-up online fabric shop.

Who this affects

Low vision covers many things: blurry sight, cataracts, glaucoma, losing central or side vision, or simply ageing eyes. Many people zoom their screen to 200% or more, and blind people use screen readers that read the page aloud.

Strong contrast also helps everyone reading a phone outside in bright Accra sunshine.

Tips for your own pages

  • Keep a contrast ratio of at least 4.5 to 1 for normal text.
  • Use rem units so text follows the reader's font settings.
  • Give every meaningful image an alt description.
  • Use alt="" for decorative images so screen readers skip them.
  • Check your page still works when zoomed to 200%.
